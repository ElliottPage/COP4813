This calculator estimates battery charge over time using a simplified exponential-decay model. Enter values for the initial charge, discharge rate, and time range, then calculate and plot the results.

y = A e-kx

A is the initial battery charge percentage, k is the discharge rate per hour, x is elapsed time in hours, and y is the estimated remaining battery charge.
